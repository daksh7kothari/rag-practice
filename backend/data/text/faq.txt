# SRM Team Robocon FAQ

Q: What is SRM Team Robocon?
A: SRM Team Robocon is the official robotics team of SRM Institute of Science and Technology. The team works on robotics, software, electronics, mechanical design, and related project work.

Q: What are the main domains in the team?
A: The main domains are software, electronics, mechanical, and management.

Q: What kind of questions are expected in recruitment?
A: The test usually covers basics, aptitude, domain fundamentals, and problem-solving. The exact questions may vary by domain and year.

Q: Do I need to bring a laptop for the test?
A: Only some domains may require a laptop, depending on the instructions shared by the team.

Q: Where is the recruitment venue?
A: The venue shared in the group was UB 5th floor.

Q: What are the test timings?
A: The timings shared in the group were 5:15 PM to 6:45 PM.

Q: Can I write the test on either day if I selected one domain?
A: Yes, if you selected one domain, you can usually attend on either of the two days, based on the team instructions shared in the group.

Q: Can I write the test for two domains?
A: Yes, candidates can usually write for up to two domains, depending on the recruitment rules shared by the team.

Q: What should I prepare for electronics?
A: Prepare class 11 and 12 basics, fundamentals of electronics, aptitude, and core concepts related to the domain.

Q: What should I prepare for coding?
A: Prepare logic building, basic algorithms, and problem-solving. Some questions may be objective and some may require written solutions.
