# SRM Team Robocon FAQ

Q: What is SRM Team Robocon?
A: SRM Team Robocon is the official robotics team of SRM Institute of Science and Technology. The team works on robotics, software, electronics, mechanical design, and related project work.

Q: What are the main domains in the team?
A: The main domains are software, electronics, mechanical, and management.

Q: What kind of questions are expected in recruitment?
A: The test usually covers basics, aptitude, domain fundamentals, and problem-solving. The exact questions may vary by domain and year.

Q: Do I need to bring a laptop for the test?
A: Only some domains may require a laptop, depending on the instructions shared by the team.

Q: Where is the recruitment venue?
A: The venue shared in the group was UB 5th floor.

Q: What are the test timings?
A: The timings shared in the group were 5:15 PM to 6:45 PM.

Q: Can I write the test on either day if I selected one domain?
A: Yes, if you selected one domain, you can usually attend on either of the two days, based on the team instructions shared in the group.

Q: Can I write the test for two domains?
A: Yes, candidates can usually write for up to two domains, depending on the recruitment rules shared by the team.

Q: What should I prepare for electronics?
A: Prepare class 11 and 12 basics, fundamentals of electronics, aptitude, and core concepts related to the domain.

Q: What should I prepare for coding?
A: Prepare logic building, basic algorithms, and problem-solving. Some questions may be objective and some may require written solutions.

Q: What domains are available in SRM Team Robocon?
A: Software, Electronics, Mechanical, and Management.

Q: Is prior robotics experience required?
A: No. Candidates are selected based on aptitude, fundamentals, willingness to learn, and problem-solving skills.

Q: Should I bring a laptop for the recruitment test?
A: Candidates are advised to bring a fully charged laptop unless otherwise instructed by the recruitment team.

Q: Is a laptop mandatory for Electronics candidates?
A: A laptop is recommended as some assessments may involve technical or programming-related activities.

Q: What programming languages should Software candidates know?
A: Basic knowledge of Python, C++, or C is beneficial.

Q: Is C programming important?
A: Yes. Understanding variables, loops, functions, arrays, pointers, and structures can be useful.

Q: Is Python important?
A: Yes. Python is widely used for automation, computer vision, machine learning, and robotics applications.

Q: What topics should Software candidates prepare?
A: Basic programming, problem solving, data structures, algorithms, Linux fundamentals, Git, and robotics basics.

Q: What topics should Electronics candidates prepare?
A: Basic electronics, circuits, Ohm's law, sensors, microcontrollers, communication protocols, and troubleshooting.

Q: What topics should Mechanical candidates prepare?
A: CAD, manufacturing processes, machine elements, mechanisms, and engineering fundamentals.

Q: What topics should Management candidates prepare?
A: Communication, leadership, organization, sponsorship, content creation, and event management.

Q: Is web development useful in Robocon?
A: Web development can be useful for creating dashboards, monitoring systems, internal tools, and team websites.

Q: What web development topics should I learn?
A: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, APIs, and basic backend concepts.

Q: Is React used in robotics?
A: React can be used for robot dashboards, telemetry visualization, monitoring systems, and internal web tools.

Q: What software tools are commonly used?
A: Python, C++, ROS, OpenCV, Git, Linux, VS Code, and simulation tools.

Q: What is ROS?
A: ROS (Robot Operating System) is a framework used for robot software development.

Q: What is OpenCV?
A: OpenCV is a computer vision library used for image processing and object detection.

Q: What is YOLO?
A: YOLO is a real-time object detection model commonly used in robotics and computer vision.

Q: What is embedded programming?
A: Embedded programming involves writing software that runs on microcontrollers and embedded devices.

Q: What microcontrollers are commonly used?
A: STM32, ESP32, Arduino, Teensy, and other embedded platforms.

Q: What communication protocols should I know?
A: UART, SPI, I2C, CAN, and Ethernet.

Q: What is PCB design?
A: PCB design is the process of designing circuit boards for electronic systems.

Q: What sensors are commonly used in robots?
A: IMUs, encoders, cameras, LiDAR, ultrasonic sensors, and limit switches.

Q: What is computer vision?
A: Computer vision enables robots to understand and process visual information from cameras.

Q: What is autonomous navigation?
A: Autonomous navigation allows robots to move and perform tasks without human intervention.

Q: What qualities are expected from candidates?
A: Curiosity, consistency, teamwork, ownership, discipline, and willingness to learn.

Q: What happens` after the written test?
A: Shortlisted candidates may be invited for interviews or further evaluation rounds.

Q: Will beginners be trained?
A: Yes. Selected candidates receive guidance, workshops, and mentoring from senior members.

Q: What is the main goal of SRM Team Robocon?
A: To design competitive robots while helping students develop technical and leadership skills.

Q: How can I prepare for recruitment?
A: Revise fundamentals of your preferred domain, practice problem solving, and learn basic robotics concepts.
